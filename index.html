How it works

Three simple steps, and the final choice is always yours.

  1. 1

    Tell us who it’s for

    Add the recipient and the occasion — a birthday, an anniversary, a leaving party, a client gift. Include a relationship and a budget if you have one.

  2. 2

    Add what you know

    Interests, themes, sizes, dislikes — or nothing at all if you’d rather not. Giftgenie works with whatever you can tell it.

  3. 3

    Get considered suggestions

    Options are matched against the person, the occasion and your budget — and each one comes with a plain-English reason why it fits.

Built for the occasions that matter

Giftgenie isn’t tied to one kind of celebration. If there’s a person, a date and a budget, it can help.

  • Children’s birthdays

    Age-appropriate ideas for whatever they’re into right now, with room to note what they already own.

  • Anniversaries

    Thoughtful options for a partner, anchored to a budget you set rather than guesswork.

  • Leaving parties

    A fitting send-off for a colleague — whether it’s from you, or a collection from the wider team.

  • Corporate gifts

    Client and team gifting that stays on-brief and on-budget, with a note of the thinking behind each pick.

In development

This is a planned feature, not a live one. It is described here for transparency about our roadmap.

  • 1. Share a link

    The occasion owner creates a Giftgenie link and sends it to guests — friends, family or colleagues.

  • 2. Guests see approved guidance

    Guests see only what the owner chooses to share: approved interests, wishlist guidance and sizes. Owner-only notes stay private.

  • 3. Reservations avoid duplicates

    A guest can reserve a gift, so two people don’t buy the same thing. Reserved gifts drop out of the list for everyone else.

Where we are today

We’d rather be clear about our stage than imply more than we can deliver.

  • In private beta

    Giftgenie is being built and tested with a small group of users. Features, wording and availability may change before launch.

  • No live retailer integrations

    We don’t yet connect to retailers, and we don’t hold live stock, pricing or availability. We don’t claim any partnerships with, or approval or endorsement from, any retailer, brand or affiliate network.

  • Privacy-first by default

    This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no third-party analytics. See our Privacy Policy for exactly what that means.
